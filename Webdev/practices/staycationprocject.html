Bear Lake – Utah

The Bear Lake

Bear Lake, nicknamed the "Caribbean of the Rockies" for its intense turquoise blue water, is one of the best locations to visit in Utah because of its stunning, shallow sandy beaches, vibrant summer boating, and unique year-round recreation. Located on the Utah-Idaho border, it offers swimming, fresh raspberry shakes, hiking, and fishing, making it ideal for families and outdoor enthusiasts.

Reasons People Visit Bear Lake:

  • Stunning Turquoise Water that looks tropical.
  • Family-Friendly Beaches with warm, shallow water.
  • Boating, jet skiing, paddleboarding, and more.
  • Famous Raspberry Shakes and local festivals.
  • Year-round activities including snowmobiling and ice fishing.

Bear Lake has a beautiful visitor center where guests can learn about the lake’s history, wildlife, and the unique turquoise color of the water. It’s a great spot to start your trip, get maps, and enjoy amazing views of the shoreline.
